## Claims we will not make

- That the model predicts growth in the environment (labels are maximal rates in culture).
- That it solves slow growth (it ranks slow organisms but rarely predicts more than a day for a new lineage).
- That the model beats gRodon given the measured temperature for new orders (it trails it there).
- That the released ranges are calibrated for newly described lineages (they were too narrow on the external set).
- Any comparison with the published gRodon or Phydon numbers from their papers (different data and splits).
- That the low-energy explanation is false in general (few organisms on the thinnest energy budgets are labeled).
