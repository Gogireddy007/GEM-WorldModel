\section{Data and code availability}
The code, the curated labels (the excluded values with their evidence and the literature strains) and the predictions for
the \NGEMPredicted GEM genomes with their errors and 80\% ranges are in a public repository whose link is withheld for
anonymous review.
The derived tables the models are trained and evaluated on and the result files behind every number, table and figure
form an archive built by \texttt{paper/scripts/make\_archive.py}, which will be deposited at Zenodo with the final
version; with it every analysis can be rerun without downloading genomes, and the Supplementary Material lists the command
behind each result. The training data come from public sources: the growth-rate table distributed with gRodon
\citep{weissman2021}, the compilation of \citet{madin2020}, the primary papers of the literature strains, GTDB
R\GTDBRelease \citep{parks2022} and the GEM catalog \citep{nayfach2021}.
